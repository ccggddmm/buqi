## The evaluation problem

Multi-turn agent evaluation asks how an interactive system behaves over an extended conversation, not merely how well it answers a single prompt.

## Aggregating outcomes

A simple illustrative aggregate is:

$$
R = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T} r_t
$$

But this mean may overlook critical errors, compounding state, or tasks with different numbers of turns.

## Questions worth exploring

- Can per-turn metrics be compared across tasks of different lengths?
- How stable is a simulated user?
- What evidence should a rubric-based judge receive?

def mean_turn_reward(rewards):
